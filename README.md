# aibysitter-incidents

Submissions for the incident board at https://aibysitting.net/Incidents: postmortems of AI coding agent changes that went wrong.

## Submit

Open an issue with the [Submit an incident](https://github.com/jbailey2900/aibysitter-incidents/issues/new?template=incident.yml) form.

## Accepted

- Incidents from your own repository or work.
- No people named. Tools and models may be named.
- No secrets or private code.
- Facts: what the agent did, the impact, how it was detected, why review and CI missed it, and the fix.

## After you submit

- The submission is reviewed. Questions are asked in the issue.
- An accepted incident is published at `https://aibysitting.net/Incidents/<number>`, credited to your GitHub username, with a link to the issue. The issue is closed with a link to the entry.
- The published text may be edited for length and format. Facts are not changed without asking.
- "Caught by" lists the aibysitter rule or check IDs that would have caught the incident, or none.

## Removal and corrections

Submitters can ask for removal, a correction, or removal of their name with the [Remove or correct an incident](https://github.com/jbailey2900/aibysitter-incidents/issues/new?template=removal.yml) form.
